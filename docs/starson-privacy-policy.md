# Privacy Policy for StarsOn

**Last Updated:** October 2026

StarsOn ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the StarsOn game on any supported platform (Android, iOS, Desktop, and Web).

StarsOn is a single-player space game. It does not require an account, and it does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
None. StarsOn has no sign-in, no chat, and no forms.

### 1.2 Your Saved Game
Your game — where your ship is, your credits, cargo, ship upgrades, and the places you have visited — is saved on your device. It is not uploaded to us. On Android, the save may be included in your device's own backup to your Google account if you have Android backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google, not by us.

### 1.3 Diagnostics and Usage Data
On Android, StarsOn uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous crash reports and aggregate usage data (for example, app version, device model, operating system version, and app-open events). This helps us diagnose stability problems and improve the game. It is not used to identify you personally, and it is not linked to an advertising ID.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited data described above only to:
- Keep your game between sessions (stored locally).
- Diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build user profiles.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your saved game is stored locally on your device; uninstalling the game deletes it. Diagnostic data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit and processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we limit collection to what is described here.

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information. Anonymous diagnostic and usage data is processed by Google Firebase on our behalf as described above. We do not share data with any other third parties.

## 5. Third-Party Services

StarsOn uses Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics) for crash reporting and anonymous usage analytics on Android. These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
