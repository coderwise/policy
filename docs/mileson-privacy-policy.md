# Privacy Policy for MilesOn

**Last Updated:** July 2026

MilesOn ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy
Policy explains how we handle information when you use the MilesOn application.

MilesOn is a car trip-meter app for EV and ICE drivers. It does not require an
account, and it does not collect your name, email, contacts, or photos.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
None. MilesOn has no sign-in and no forms.

### 1.2 Location
MilesOn uses your device's location to estimate the distance you drive for each trip
meter. On Android, when your phone's Bluetooth connects to your car, MilesOn
automatically starts recording GPS-estimated distance in the background and stops when
Bluetooth disconnects, so meters keep counting driven distance across every trip
without you opening the app. Location is used on-device to calculate distance; we do
not store or transmit your location to our own servers.

### 1.3 Trip Meter Data
Trip meters you create (names, reset history) are stored locally on your device. This
data stays on your device and is not uploaded to us.

### 1.4 Vehicle Diagnostic Data (OBD2)
MilesOn optionally connects over Bluetooth to a user-supplied OBD2 adapter plugged
into your vehicle's diagnostic port. When connected, it reads standard and
vehicle-specific diagnostic data — odometer reading, speed, and (on supported EVs)
battery state of charge and charging status — to provide a more accurate,
tire-size-independent distance source and to detect charging sessions. This data is
processed on-device only, used to calculate distance and charging intervals for your
trip meters, and is not stored on or transmitted to our servers.

### 1.5 Diagnostics and Usage Data
On Android, MilesOn uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to collect
anonymous crash reports and aggregate usage data (for example, app version, device
model, operating system version, and app-open events). This helps us diagnose
stability problems and improve the app. It is not used to identify you personally.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited data described above only to:
- Estimate driven distance for your trip meters, including automatic background
  recording triggered by your car's Bluetooth connection.
- Read vehicle diagnostic data from a connected OBD2 adapter, when you choose to pair
  one, for a more accurate distance source and to detect charging sessions.
- Keep your trip meters and preferences applied between launches (stored locally).
- Diagnose crashes and improve app stability and performance.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build user profiles.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Trip meter data and preferences are stored locally on your device. Diagnostic data sent
to Firebase is processed by Google in accordance with
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). No method of
transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we limit collection to what is described
here.

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information. Anonymous diagnostic and usage data is
processed by Google Firebase on our behalf as described above. We do not share data
with any other third parties.

## 5. Third-Party Services

MilesOn uses Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics) for crash reporting and
anonymous usage analytics, and on-device location services to estimate driven
distance. These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle
data.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect
personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes
by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
